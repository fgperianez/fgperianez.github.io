Privacy policy

Personal Drive Sync is used only by its owner.

Data accessed. With the owner's consent, the application reads and writes files in the owner's own Google Drive. It does not access any other Google account or any other person's data.

Data storage and sharing. Files are transferred directly between the owner's devices and the owner's Google Drive. Nothing is stored on third-party servers, shared with anyone, or used for any other purpose.

Revoking access. The owner can revoke the application's access at any time from their Google Account permissions page.

Contact: the repository owner at github.com/fgperianez.
